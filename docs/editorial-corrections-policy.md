# GCX Editorial Corrections And Updates Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026.

GCX publishes original gaming, trading-card, entertainment, and collector-market coverage. This policy explains how GCX labels sources, updates living guides, and corrects errors as the newsroom grows.

## Source Labels

- Official information should be labeled when it comes directly from a publisher, platform holder, trading-card company, retailer, event organizer, or other primary source.
- Verified reporting should be attributed to reputable secondary sources and should not be rewritten as official confirmation.
- Reported or unofficial information should stay qualified until a primary source confirms it.
- Rumors, leaks, and unverified claims should not appear in core guides unless they are editorially necessary and explicitly labeled.
- Presale asking prices should never be described as settled market value.

## Article Metadata

- Newsroom articles should include source links, source count, editorial owner, claim status, last reviewed or last updated date, and an update policy.
- Living guides and trackers should preserve their URL and update the existing article instead of publishing replacement duplicates.
- Source links should be grouped cleanly near the article bottom so body copy stays readable.
- Material updates should be reflected in the visible article metadata.

## Corrections

- Factual errors should be corrected in the existing article.
- Material corrections should include a brief note explaining what changed.
- Minor typo, formatting, and style fixes do not need a correction note unless they changed meaning.
- If a story is based on reported information that later changes, GCX updates the article and preserves the distinction between official facts, verified reporting, and analysis.
- If a source removes or changes information, GCX updates the article with the new context instead of leaving outdated claims unqualified.

## Review Cadence

- Living event hubs should be reviewed before, during, and after the event window.
- Price and preorder trackers should be reviewed when official product pages, retailers, or reputable reports change.
- Rumor-sensitive stories should be reviewed when the primary source publishes an update.
- Evergreen guides should be reviewed when source links break, product details change, or new official information becomes available.

## Reader Feedback

Readers can use GCX Contact to flag corrections, source issues, broken links, or article updates that need review. GCX prioritizes corrections that affect safety, pricing, availability, legal/trust claims, or buying decisions.
