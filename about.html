About GCX

A gaming and collector platform built in public.

Games Exchange combines original coverage, game libraries, trading-card indexes, community tools, and a future verified marketplace.

Editorial

Useful coverage over article volume

GCX prioritizes trackers, databases, and reporting that can stay useful over time. Major stories are updated instead of replaced when a living hub makes more sense.

Collectors

Cards, games, and consoles in one place

The database work starts with Pokemon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh, and game libraries, then connects those records to community and marketplace intent.

Marketplace

Trading is not live yet

The exchange remains a beta waitlist until account verification, listing rules, payment-provider policies, moderation, and dispute workflows are ready.

Trust

Clear sourcing and clear limits

GCX separates official sources, verified reporting, reported claims, and rumors. Pricing coverage should label MSRP, retail, presale, and market prices clearly.
